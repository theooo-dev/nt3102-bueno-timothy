IRONCLAD FITNESS — INTERNAL SYSTEM

One dashboard for the front desk, built just for Ironclad.

FitPulse is Ironclad Fitness's own membership and attendance system — member records, QR-based check-in, equipment upkeep, and payments, all in one place, with automatic email reminders before a membership lapses.

WHY FITPULSE

Built around what a front-desk day actually looks like

Digitize member records

Register, edit, and search member profiles instead of paper folders and loose spreadsheets.

Track plans and renewals

See active and expired memberships at a glance, with full renewal history per member.

Automatic reminders

Email members 3–7 days before expiration, and again once a membership has lapsed.

QR-BASED CHECK-IN

Attendance that logs itself

Each member's unique QR code is scanned at the door. FitPulse timestamps the visit and adds it straight to their attendance history — no sign-in sheet required.

  • ✓ One QR code per member, generated automatically
  • ✓ Entry is timestamped the moment it's scanned
  • ✓ Full attendance history searchable by member

WHAT IT MANAGES

Every part of daily operations, in one place

Register new members, store contact details, edit records, and view full profiles at any time.

Assign plans, record start and expiration dates, and view active, expired, and renewal history in one screen.

Track equipment by category and condition (Good, Needs Maintenance, Under Repair, Out of Service), log repair history, and get notified when inspections are due.

Generate a unique QR code per member, scan on entry, and automatically record check-in date and time.

Log cash or GCash payments manually, with amount and date, and browse full payment history per member. No online payment gateway is processed by the system.

Automatically flag memberships nearing expiration and send reminder emails, plus a separate notice once a membership has expired.

Find any member by name, member ID, or contact information, and open their complete record instantly.
